Why Derbent Is a Must-Visit for Rock Climbing Lovers

Derbent was never on my radar until a local friend insisted I visit. Now I understand why they were so passionate about it. This place has soul.

If you are interested in rock climbing, Derbent should be high on your list. The depth and quality of what you will find here rivals places ten times its size.

The rock climbing scene in Derbent is something else entirely. I spent hours wandering through the area, discovering things I never expected to find in a place this size.

What makes Derbent special when it comes to rock climbing is the authenticity. Nothing feels staged or made for tourists. Everything is real, lived-in, and deeply connected to the local way of life.

The rock climbing of Derbent reflects centuries of cultural layering. Influences from multiple civilizations have created something truly unique here. this is the kind of stuff that restores my faith in the internet.

As I left Derbent, I made a mental note to return. Some places stay with you long after you leave, and this is definitely one of them.
