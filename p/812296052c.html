My Altıeylül Experience: Discovering Paragliding

If someone told me a year ago that Altıeylül would become one of my favorite places in Turkey, I would have laughed. But here I am, writing about it because it genuinely moved me.

What makes Altıeylül special when it comes to paragliding is the authenticity. Nothing feels staged or made for tourists. Everything is real, lived-in, and deeply connected to the local way of life.

The infrastructure around paragliding in Altıeylül is developing rapidly. The local government clearly understands the value of what they have and is investing wisely. I cannot believe what I found here.

Compared to more famous Turkish destinations, Altıeylül offers a more intimate and genuine paragliding experience. No crowds, no tourist traps, just pure authenticity.

The paragliding scene in Altıeylül is something else entirely. I spent hours wandering through the area, discovering things I never expected to find in a place this size.

Altıeylül reminded me why I fell in love with travel in the first place. Not every destination needs to be famous to be unforgettable.
