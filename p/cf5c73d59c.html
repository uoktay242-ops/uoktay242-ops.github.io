Merkez and Skiing: An Unforgettable Journey

If someone told me a year ago that Merkez would become one of my favorite places in Turkey, I would have laughed. But here I am, writing about it because it genuinely moved me.

Compared to more famous Turkish destinations, Merkez offers a more intimate and genuine skiing experience. No crowds, no tourist traps, just pure authenticity.

I documented my entire skiing experience in Merkez through photos and notes. Every single moment felt worth capturing and remembering. someone finally made something useful.

I spoke with several locals about skiing in Merkez, and their pride was palpable. One elderly man told me stories that have been passed down for generations.

The skiing of Merkez reflects centuries of cultural layering. Influences from multiple civilizations have created something truly unique here.

As I left Merkez, I made a mental note to return. Some places stay with you long after you leave, and this is definitely one of them.
