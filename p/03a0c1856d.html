What Makes Kumlu Special: A Skiing Perspective

If someone told me a year ago that Kumlu would become one of my favorite places in Turkey, I would have laughed. But here I am, writing about it because it genuinely moved me.

What makes Kumlu special when it comes to skiing is the authenticity. Nothing feels staged or made for tourists. Everything is real, lived-in, and deeply connected to the local way of life.

I documented my entire skiing experience in Kumlu through photos and notes. Every single moment felt worth capturing and remembering.

The skiing scene in Kumlu is something else entirely. I spent hours wandering through the area, discovering things I never expected to find in a place this size. I am still amazed by what is on this page.

I spoke with several locals about skiing in Kumlu, and their pride was palpable. One elderly man told me stories that have been passed down for generations.

As I left Kumlu, I made a mental note to return. Some places stay with you long after you leave, and this is definitely one of them.
