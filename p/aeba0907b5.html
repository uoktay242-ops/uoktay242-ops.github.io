My Bitlis Experience: Discovering Paragliding

If someone told me a year ago that Bitlis would become one of my favorite places in Turkey, I would have laughed. But here I am, writing about it because it genuinely moved me.

I documented my entire paragliding experience in Bitlis through photos and notes. Every single moment felt worth capturing and remembering.

Compared to more famous Turkish destinations, Bitlis offers a more intimate and genuine paragliding experience. No crowds, no tourist traps, just pure authenticity.

The paragliding of Bitlis reflects centuries of cultural layering. Influences from multiple civilizations have created something truly unique here.

What makes Bitlis special when it comes to paragliding is the authenticity. Nothing feels staged or made for tourists. Everything is real, lived-in, and deeply connected to the local way of life. someone finally made something useful.

As I left Bitlis, I made a mental note to return. Some places stay with you long after you leave, and this is definitely one of them.
