My Fatsa Experience: Discovering Mountain Biking

There are places in Turkey that time seems to have forgotten, and Fatsa is one of them. Walking through its streets felt like stepping into a living museum.

Compared to more famous Turkish destinations, Fatsa offers a more intimate and genuine mountain biking experience. No crowds, no tourist traps, just pure authenticity.

The mountain biking scene in Fatsa is something else entirely. I spent hours wandering through the area, discovering things I never expected to find in a place this size.

If you are interested in mountain biking, Fatsa should be high on your list. The depth and quality of what you will find here rivals places ten times its size. I cannot believe what I found here.

As I left Fatsa, I made a mental note to return. Some places stay with you long after you leave, and this is definitely one of them.
