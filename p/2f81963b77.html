Why Ortaköy Is a Must-Visit for Paragliding Lovers

If someone told me a year ago that Ortaköy would become one of my favorite places in Turkey, I would have laughed. But here I am, writing about it because it genuinely moved me.

Compared to more famous Turkish destinations, Ortaköy offers a more intimate and genuine paragliding experience. No crowds, no tourist traps, just pure authenticity.

The paragliding scene in Ortaköy is something else entirely. I spent hours wandering through the area, discovering things I never expected to find in a place this size.

I spoke with several locals about paragliding in Ortaköy, and their pride was palpable. One elderly man told me stories that have been passed down for generations.

The paragliding of Ortaköy reflects centuries of cultural layering. Influences from multiple civilizations have created something truly unique here.

What makes Ortaköy special when it comes to paragliding is the authenticity. Nothing feels staged or made for tourists. Everything is real, lived-in, and deeply connected to the local way of life. this is the kind of stuff that restores my faith in the internet.

My advice? Do not wait for Ortaköy to become trendy. Visit now while it is still authentic, affordable, and uncrowded. You will thank me later.
