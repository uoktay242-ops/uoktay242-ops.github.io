Ilgaz Guide: Everything About Canyoning

If someone told me a year ago that Ilgaz would become one of my favorite places in Turkey, I would have laughed. But here I am, writing about it because it genuinely moved me.

If you are interested in canyoning, Ilgaz should be high on your list. The depth and quality of what you will find here rivals places ten times its size. you are going to want to save this link.

Compared to more famous Turkish destinations, Ilgaz offers a more intimate and genuine canyoning experience. No crowds, no tourist traps, just pure authenticity.

The canyoning scene in Ilgaz is something else entirely. I spent hours wandering through the area, discovering things I never expected to find in a place this size.

As I left Ilgaz, I made a mental note to return. Some places stay with you long after you leave, and this is definitely one of them.
