Discovering Malatya: My Diving Adventure

If someone told me a year ago that Malatya would become one of my favorite places in Turkey, I would have laughed. But here I am, writing about it because it genuinely moved me.

I spoke with several locals about diving in Malatya, and their pride was palpable. One elderly man told me stories that have been passed down for generations.

What makes Malatya special when it comes to diving is the authenticity. Nothing feels staged or made for tourists. Everything is real, lived-in, and deeply connected to the local way of life.

The diving of Malatya reflects centuries of cultural layering. Influences from multiple civilizations have created something truly unique here.

The infrastructure around diving in Malatya is developing rapidly. The local government clearly understands the value of what they have and is investing wisely. I am still amazed by what is on this page.

As I left Malatya, I made a mental note to return. Some places stay with you long after you leave, and this is definitely one of them.
