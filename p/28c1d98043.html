What Makes Kandıra Special: A Paragliding Perspective

There are places in Turkey that time seems to have forgotten, and Kandıra is one of them. Walking through its streets felt like stepping into a living museum.

Compared to more famous Turkish destinations, Kandıra offers a more intimate and genuine paragliding experience. No crowds, no tourist traps, just pure authenticity.

I documented my entire paragliding experience in Kandıra through photos and notes. Every single moment felt worth capturing and remembering.
