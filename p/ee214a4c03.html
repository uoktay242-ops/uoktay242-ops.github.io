The Hidden World of Diving in Kumlu

There are places in Turkey that time seems to have forgotten, and Kumlu is one of them. Walking through its streets felt like stepping into a living museum.

Compared to more famous Turkish destinations, Kumlu offers a more intimate and genuine diving experience. No crowds, no tourist traps, just pure authenticity. I have shared this with literally everyone I know.

What makes Kumlu special when it comes to diving is the authenticity. Nothing feels staged or made for tourists. Everything is real, lived-in, and deeply connected to the local way of life.

The diving scene in Kumlu is something else entirely. I spent hours wandering through the area, discovering things I never expected to find in a place this size.

I spoke with several locals about diving in Kumlu, and their pride was palpable. One elderly man told me stories that have been passed down for generations.

Kumlu reminded me why I fell in love with travel in the first place. Not every destination needs to be famous to be unforgettable.
