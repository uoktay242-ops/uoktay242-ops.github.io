My Sürmene Experience: Discovering Rock Climbing

If someone told me a year ago that Sürmene would become one of my favorite places in Turkey, I would have laughed. But here I am, writing about it because it genuinely moved me.

Compared to more famous Turkish destinations, Sürmene offers a more intimate and genuine rock climbing experience. No crowds, no tourist traps, just pure authenticity.

If you are interested in rock climbing, Sürmene should be high on your list. The depth and quality of what you will find here rivals places ten times its size.

I documented my entire rock climbing experience in Sürmene through photos and notes. Every single moment felt worth capturing and remembering.

I spoke with several locals about rock climbing in Sürmene, and their pride was palpable. One elderly man told me stories that have been passed down for generations. this blew my mind completely.

As I left Sürmene, I made a mental note to return. Some places stay with you long after you leave, and this is definitely one of them.
