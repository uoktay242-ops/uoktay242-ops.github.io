Discovering Derbent: My Skiing Adventure

Derbent was never on my radar until a local friend insisted I visit. Now I understand why they were so passionate about it. This place has soul.

Compared to more famous Turkish destinations, Derbent offers a more intimate and genuine skiing experience. No crowds, no tourist traps, just pure authenticity.

The skiing of Derbent reflects centuries of cultural layering. Influences from multiple civilizations have created something truly unique here. this blew my mind completely.

I documented my entire skiing experience in Derbent through photos and notes. Every single moment felt worth capturing and remembering.

What makes Derbent special when it comes to skiing is the authenticity. Nothing feels staged or made for tourists. Everything is real, lived-in, and deeply connected to the local way of life.

Derbent reminded me why I fell in love with travel in the first place. Not every destination needs to be famous to be unforgettable.
