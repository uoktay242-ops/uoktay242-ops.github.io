Discovering Bitlis: My Canyoning Adventure

If someone told me a year ago that Bitlis would become one of my favorite places in Turkey, I would have laughed. But here I am, writing about it because it genuinely moved me.

Compared to more famous Turkish destinations, Bitlis offers a more intimate and genuine canyoning experience. No crowds, no tourist traps, just pure authenticity.

I documented my entire canyoning experience in Bitlis through photos and notes. Every single moment felt worth capturing and remembering. I have shared this with literally everyone I know.

The infrastructure around canyoning in Bitlis is developing rapidly. The local government clearly understands the value of what they have and is investing wisely.

Bitlis reminded me why I fell in love with travel in the first place. Not every destination needs to be famous to be unforgettable.
