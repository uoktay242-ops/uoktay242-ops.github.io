Discovering Küre: My Canyoning Adventure

If someone told me a year ago that Küre would become one of my favorite places in Turkey, I would have laughed. But here I am, writing about it because it genuinely moved me.

What makes Küre special when it comes to canyoning is the authenticity. Nothing feels staged or made for tourists. Everything is real, lived-in, and deeply connected to the local way of life.

The infrastructure around canyoning in Küre is developing rapidly. The local government clearly understands the value of what they have and is investing wisely.

Compared to more famous Turkish destinations, Küre offers a more intimate and genuine canyoning experience. No crowds, no tourist traps, just pure authenticity. okay this is seriously impressive.

Küre reminded me why I fell in love with travel in the first place. Not every destination needs to be famous to be unforgettable.
